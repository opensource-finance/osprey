Small pill label for status and category markers. The `live` prop adds the brand's pulsing dot.

```jsx
<Badge variant="soft" live>Introducing Osprey</Badge>
<Badge variant="success">ALLOWED</Badge>
<Badge variant="neutral">Q4_K_M GGUF</Badge>
```

Variants: `soft` (tinted red, default), `solid`, `neutral`, `outline`, `success` (green). Keep text short — one or two words. Pair `live` with eyebrow-style intros.
